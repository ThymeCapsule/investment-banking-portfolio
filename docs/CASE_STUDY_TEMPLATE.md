# Case Study Publication Template

Use this structure for each new valuation or transaction case. Adapt the technical sections to the company and method; do not force an industrial DCF template onto a bank or mining company.

## 1. Case question

State the decision or valuation question in one or two sentences. Define the perspective: standalone equity value, asset NAV, acquisition price, financing decision or transaction impact.

## 2. Executive conclusion

Summarise the model-derived result, valuation range, reference date and the two or three factors that matter most. State whether the output is a base case, scenario, partial valuation or fully reconciled result. Avoid unsupported buy / sell language.

## 3. Company and transaction context

Describe the business, segment mix, capital structure and relevant transaction terms. Include the valuation date and reporting periods.

## 4. Historical financial analysis

Show at least three years where available. Reconcile reported and adjusted measures. Explain one-offs, accounting changes, seasonality and unusual working-capital movements.

## 5. Forecast methodology

Identify the operating drivers and show how they translate into revenue, margins, earnings, cash flow, capital requirements or mine-level cash flows. Distinguish guidance from analyst assumptions.

## 6. Valuation / transaction analysis

Explain the method and key formulas. Reconcile enterprise value to equity value or asset value to equity NAV. For M&A, show offer terms, sources & uses, financing, purchase accounting and pro forma effects where relevant.

## 7. Sensitivities and scenarios

Show the variables that materially affect the conclusion. The centre of each sensitivity must tie to the base case. Include a downside that tests the core thesis rather than merely moving one input by a small amount.

## 8. Risks, limitations and diligence questions

Separate model limitations from business risks. State what is missing, what is estimated, and what would change the conclusion.

## 9. Sources and assumptions

Provide a dated source register. Every material hard-coded figure should be traceable to a primary source or explicitly identified as an analyst assumption.

## 10. Model checks and release status

List formula, accounting, valuation, transaction and source checks. State who reviewed the model, what was checked and what remains open. A check should never be described as passed unless it has actually been run.
