## Data and method caveats
Company disclosures are primary for financial information. Market-data pages are used only for dated price/rate snapshots. Damodaran ERP inputs are estimates and are not market prices. Peer comparisons are indicative and not perfectly like-for-like. Workbook notes identify assumptions that are not directly disclosed by the companies.
