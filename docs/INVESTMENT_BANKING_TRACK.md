# Investment Banking Track — Evidence and Development Priorities

**Purpose:** define the investment-banking analytical capabilities this portfolio is intended to evidence, identify the work already represented, and distinguish it from capabilities not yet demonstrated.

This is a development framework for an independent portfolio. It is not a claim of Goldman Sachs affiliation, investment-bank employment, deal execution experience or compliance with any firm's internal modelling standards.

## 2. What the Frasers / Accent flagship case must demonstrate

The transaction case is the highest-priority project because it brings valuation, financial analysis and deal mechanics together.

### Transaction overview
- Buyer and target profiles, strategic rationale and transaction timetable.
- Offer terms, undisturbed price, premium, existing ownership and fully diluted share count.
- Equity value, enterprise value and consideration bridge.

### Standalone analysis
- Historical financial statements and adjustments.
- Revenue, gross margin, operating costs, lease treatment, capex, D&A and working-capital drivers.
- Standalone DCF and trading comparables, with consistent accounting and valuation dates.
- Downside, base and upside operating scenarios.

### Deal mechanics
- Sources & uses and funding mix.
- Cash available, new debt, refinancing, transaction fees and interest costs.
- Purchase-price allocation, identifiable intangible assets, deferred tax and goodwill.
- Pro forma opening balance sheet and combined income statement.
- Cost and revenue synergies, phasing, probability, implementation costs and tax effects.
- EPS accretion / dilution by year, with cash, debt and scrip funding sensitivities.
- Break-even synergy and EPS-neutral offer price.

### Controls
- Sources equal uses.
- Pro forma balance sheet balances.
- Debt, cash, shares and interest roll forward.
- Purchase consideration reconciles to the bidder's statement.
- Sensitivity centres tie to the base case.
- Every material input has a source, date, unit and classification.

## 3. Quality bar for every case

A reviewer should be able to open the workbook and answer, without asking the author:
1. What question is the model answering?
2. What is reported fact versus analyst assumption?
3. Where did each important input come from?
4. How does an operating assumption flow into valuation?
5. What happens when a key assumption changes?
6. What checks prove that the schedules reconcile?
7. What remains uncertain and could change the conclusion?

A visually polished workbook does not compensate for weak source work, opaque formulas or an unsupported conclusion.

## 4. Recruitment-facing presentation

Use the portfolio to demonstrate evidence, not to make unsupported claims.

Recommended positioning:
- **Investment banking focus:** M&A, corporate finance, valuation and financial modelling.
- **Demonstrated methods:** DCF, asset NAV, residual-income valuation and preliminary takeover / accretion analysis.
- **Tools:** Excel, Python and R, limited to work actually completed and explainable in an interview.
- **Current development:** integrated merger modelling, financing schedules, purchase accounting and fully reconciled comparables.

Do not claim:
- Live deal execution or client advisory experience from independent case studies.
- A completed merger model while sources & uses, purchase accounting and pro forma statements are absent.
- A validated valuation while workbook and summary outputs disagree.
- That a portfolio guarantees an interview or meets a particular bank's hiring threshold.
