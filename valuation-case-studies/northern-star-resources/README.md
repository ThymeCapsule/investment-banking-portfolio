# Northern Star Resources — Forecast-Period Asset NAV

## Case question

What is the discounted value of the explicit FY27–FY36 cash-flow scenario for Northern Star's existing operations and the separate Hemi development scenario?

## Methodology

The model discounts forecast-period asset cash flows using an 8.0% nominal AUD discount rate. The current approach assigns **zero residual value** beyond FY36. It is therefore a finite-horizon forecast-period NAV, not a complete reserve-life NAV or a going-concern valuation.

## Scope and limitations

The 31 March 2026 Group Ore Reserve is reported as 28.419Moz, including 5.508Moz at Hemi. The modelled production horizon ends in FY36 and does not assign value to reserve tonnes outside that horizon. This omission is material: the result should not be interpreted as the value of all reported reserves.

FY27 production, AISC and capital expenditure are anchored to company guidance midpoints. FY28–FY36 production profiles, costs, capex and Hemi ramp-up are analyst scenarios, not reserve-engineered mine plans.

## Required analytical work

1. Build asset-level production, grade, recovery, throughput and mine-life schedules from technical disclosures.
2. Model realised gold prices, FX, royalties, tax and working capital consistently by jurisdiction.
3. Separate sustaining and growth capital; confirm whether sustaining capital is already included in AISC to avoid double counting.
4. Model rehabilitation, closure and environmental liabilities at asset level.
5. Reconcile asset-level cash flows to group net debt, corporate costs, hedging and diluted shares.
6. Explain the nominal/real basis and currency of the discount rate.
7. Add a reserve-life case or explicitly retain the finite-horizon label; do not introduce a terminal value without a defensible operating basis.
