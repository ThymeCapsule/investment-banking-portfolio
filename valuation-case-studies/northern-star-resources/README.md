## Case question

What is the discounted value of the explicit FY27–FY36 cash-flow scenario for Northern Star's existing operations and the separate Hemi development scenario?

## Methodology and model architecture

The model discounts forecast asset cash flows for existing operations and Hemi using an 8.0% nominal AUD discount rate. The workbook includes a FY27 guidance bridge, mine-life diagnostics, input schedule, NAV calculation, gold-price / FX and discount-rate / residual-value sensitivities, one-page output, reserve reconciliation, model checks, audit log and source register.

FY27 volumes and AISC use published guidance midpoints. FY28–FY36 production, costs, capex and sustaining capital are analyst scenarios. Hemi first-gold timing, ramp-up, AISC, D&A and capex remain scenario assumptions pending further project-level evidence.

## Scope and limitations

The base case assigns zero value to cash flows beyond FY36. The workbook's reserve reconciliation identifies approximately 6.436Moz of existing-operations reserves and 2.658Moz of Hemi reserves remaining beyond the forecast horizon. These reserve tonnes and potential future cash flows are excluded from the base-case value.

The 8.0% discount rate is an analyst judgement, not a market-implied CAPM output. The workbook contains a CAPM cross-check, but that cross-check is not used as the NAV discount rate. The model does not yet contain a completed peer EV/EBITDA, P/NAV or precedent-transaction analysis.

## Model checks and open items

The workbook's mechanical checks pass for the asset NAV bridge, equity NAV bridge, per-share bridge, existing and Hemi reserve reconciliation, FY27 production guidance bridge and reported reserve total bridge.

Principal outstanding items:

1. Build asset-level production, grade, recovery, throughput and mine-life schedules from technical disclosures.
2. Validate Hemi FID, commissioning, first-gold timing and ramp-up against updated studies and approvals.
3. Model realised gold prices, FX, royalties, tax and working capital consistently by jurisdiction.
4. Separate sustaining and growth capital; confirm sustaining capex is not double counted in AISC.
5. Model rehabilitation, closure and environmental liabilities at asset level.
6. Reconcile asset cash flows to group net debt, corporate costs, hedging and diluted shares.
7. Build a defensible reserve-life case or retain the explicit finite-horizon label; do not introduce a residual value without a supportable operating basis.

## Interpretation

The A$13.89/share result is a forecast-period NAV under the stated FY27–FY36 assumptions and zero-tail convention. It should not be represented as the value of all reported reserves or as a complete going-concern valuation.
