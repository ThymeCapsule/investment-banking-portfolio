## Case question
What is the discounted value of the explicit FY27-FY36 cash-flow scenario for Northern Star's existing operations and the separate Hemi development scenario?

## Current model outputs
- Asset NAV / EV: **A$19.93bn**.
- Equity NAV: **A$19.77bn**.
- Forecast-period NAV: **A$13.89 per share**.
- Market price reference: **A$25.85** (9 Nov 2026).
- Discount rate: **8.0% nominal AUD**; residual value: **zero**.

## Important interpretation
This is not a full reserve-life NAV. The 31 March 2026 Group Ore Reserve is 28.419Moz, including 5.508Moz at Hemi; the derived ex-Hemi reserve base is therefore approximately 22.911Moz. The explicit forecast ends in FY36 and no value is assigned to the remaining reserve tail: approximately 6.436Moz of existing-operations reserves and 2.658Moz of Hemi reserves. The A$13.89/share figure must not be called fair value or a target price.

## Model construction and open diligence
FY27 production, AISC and capex are aligned to September-quarter guidance midpoints. Total growth capital includes Hemi; the workbook splits this into A$1.635bn ex-Hemi plus A$225m Hemi capex. Later mine plans, cost curves, Hemi schedule and costs, site-specific royalties, taxes, closure liabilities and a reserve-life terminal framework require further work.
