# DoorDash / Instacart — Stock-and-Cash Strategic Merger

<sub>TRANSACTION CASE STUDY · ANNOUNCED 29 OCTOBER 2026 · ANALYSIS IN PROGRESS</sub>

## Case question

Does DoorDash's proposed acquisition of Instacart create sufficient strategic and financial value to justify the offer consideration, financing burden and regulatory execution risk?

DoorDash stated that it intends to retain the Instacart brand, enterprise technology products and app after closing. Both companies continue to operate independently until completion.

## Why this is a strong merger-modelling case

1. **Mixed consideration:** The cash / stock election and 25% stock cap require a proration and share-issuance model rather than a simple fixed-price acquisition calculation.
2. **Financing and ratings:** The cash component is expected to be funded through cash and debt, supported by a 364-day bridge commitment. The model should test refinancing, interest expense, leverage and DoorDash's stated investment-grade capital-structure objective.
3. **Synergies and strategic overlap:** The companies cite merchant reach, grocery and local-commerce distribution, membership benefits and broader consumer choice. These claims need a bottom-up synergy build and implementation-cost schedule.
4. **Accretion / dilution:** Model standalone EPS, financing costs, new shares, purchase accounting, synergies and amortisation separately.
5. **Regulatory risk:** The transaction requires shareholder and regulatory approvals, including in the US, Canada and Israel. Closing timing and probability should be scenario-tested.
6. **Value allocation:** Separate value accruing to Instacart shareholders from value creation or dilution for DoorDash shareholders.
