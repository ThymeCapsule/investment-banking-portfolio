# Intuit / Mailchimp — Strategic Divestiture and Carve-Out

<sub>TRANSACTION CASE STUDY · ANNOUNCED 19 NOVEMBER 2026 · ANALYSIS IN PROGRESS</sub>

## Case question

Why is Intuit divesting Mailchimp to Bending Spoons at an enterprise value of US$3.68bn, and how should a buyer assess the standalone economics of the business, separation requirements and value creation opportunity?

## Transaction snapshot

| Item | Announced terms |
|---|---:|
| Seller | Intuit Inc. |
| Buyer | Bending Spoons S.p.A. |
| Asset / business | Mailchimp |
| Announcement | 19 November 2026 |
| Enterprise value | **US$3.68bn** |
| Consideration | All cash |
| Seller reinvestment | Intuit to invest **US$368m** of proceeds into newly issued Bending Spoons equity |
| Transaction perimeter | Acquisition of 100% of The Rocket Science Group, LLC and other Mailchimp business assets and liabilities |
| Expected closing | Early 2027 |
| Conditions | Regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions |

The transaction was unanimously approved by Bending Spoons' board. Citi and J.P. Morgan are co-lead financial advisers to Bending Spoons; Goldman Sachs Bank Europe SE, Succursale Italia and UniCredit are financial advisers on the transaction.

## Why this is an attractive modelling case

This is a carve-out and strategic divestiture rather than a conventional public-company merger. The work should focus on the standalone economics and transaction perimeter, not merely divide enterprise value by a headline EBITDA figure.

1. **Standalone financials:** Reconstruct Mailchimp's revenue, gross profit, operating expenses, customer economics and cash generation from the disclosures available. Intuit's consolidated reporting may not provide a complete standalone income statement or balance sheet.
2. **Carve-out adjustments:** Identify stranded costs, shared technology, corporate services, employee arrangements, data, intellectual property, contracts and transitional service requirements.
3. **Purchase-price analysis:** Reconcile enterprise value to assumed debt, cash, working capital and other debt-like items once the transaction perimeter and purchase agreement are available.
4. **Buyer returns:** Build a standalone operating forecast, DCF and illustrative LBO with explicit leverage, financing cost, exit assumptions and return sensitivities.
5. **Seller economics:** Assess Intuit's cash proceeds, US$368m reinvestment and continuing exposure to Bending Spoons' equity value.
6. **Strategic rationale:** Evaluate Mailchimp's brand, customer base, product position and potential operating investment against competitive pressure and execution risk.

## Planned deliverables

- Public-source financial fact base and transaction timeline.
- Standalone historical financial reconstruction with a clear disclosure-gap register.
- Purchase-price and enterprise-value bridge.
- Standalone operating model and DCF.
- Illustrative sources & uses and sponsor LBO, with assumptions separated from disclosed terms.
- Carve-out costs, stranded-cost and transitional-services sensitivities.
- Intuit seller-proceeds and rollover-investment analysis.
- Investment committee summary with key diligence questions.

## Current status and limitations

**Status: announced transaction fact base established; independent financial reconstruction and valuation not yet completed.**

The US$3.68bn figure is the announced enterprise value, not equity value. The public announcement does not by itself provide a complete standalone Mailchimp financial history, purchase-price allocation, detailed working-capital mechanism, debt-like item schedule, financing structure or full separation-cost estimate. No purchase multiple, DCF value, sponsor IRR or money-on-money return is published in this case study.

The US$368m reinvestment is a seller investment into newly issued Bending Spoons equity; it should not be presented as a reduction in the stated enterprise value without examining the definitive transaction documentation and funds flow.
