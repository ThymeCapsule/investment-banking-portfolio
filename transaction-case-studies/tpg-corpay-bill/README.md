# TPG / Corpay / BILL — Take-Private and Strategic Co-Investment

<sub>LIVE TRANSACTION CASE STUDY · ANNOUNCED 19 NOVEMBER 2026 · ANALYSIS IN PROGRESS</sub>

## Case question

How should an investor assess the $6.0bn take-private of BILL by TPG in partnership with Corpay? The case examines the purchase-price bridge, sponsor and strategic-investor economics, the minority co-investment structure, financing and returns, and Corpay's strategic rationale.

## Why this transaction is worth analysing

The structure creates several distinct questions that a simple headline purchase multiple would miss:

1. **Sponsor / strategic consortium:** TPG will hold the majority interest while Corpay makes a substantial minority investment. Ownership, governance, control and economic participation need to be modelled separately.
2. **Strategic rationale:** Corpay described the investment as part of a strategic rotation into corporate payments and expects it to be accretive to 2027 cash EPS. That claim requires independent testing against funding cost, earnings attribution and the accounting treatment of the investment.
3. **Option economics:** Corpay's stated option to acquire 100% in 2030 creates a second valuation and control question. The option should not be treated as a guaranteed future acquisition.
4. **Take-private valuation:** The US$63.25 cash offer should be assessed against unaffected trading, standalone valuation, trading comparables and relevant transaction evidence.
5. **Sponsor returns:** The majority investor's equity contribution, leverage, fees, cash generation, exit assumptions and return sensitivities need to be separated from Corpay's minority-investment economics.

## Planned analytical work

- Reconstruct the unaffected share-price reference and offer-premium calculation using dated market data.
- Build the fully diluted equity value and enterprise-value bridge, including cash, debt, leases and other debt-like items.
- Analyse BILL's historical financial performance, recurring revenue, gross margin, operating leverage, cash conversion and working-capital profile.
- Build a standalone operating forecast and DCF, with explicit assumptions and downside sensitivities.
- Prepare trading comparables and precedent-transaction analysis using consistently dated and defined metrics.
- Build transaction sources and uses and an illustrative sponsor LBO, clearly separating disclosed terms from analyst assumptions.
- Model TPG's majority ownership and Corpay's minority investment without assuming full consolidation or full earnings attribution absent support.
- Assess Corpay's investment funding, expected cash-EPS impact and the 2030 option as separate scenarios.
- Identify execution, regulatory, financing, customer, competition and technology risks.
